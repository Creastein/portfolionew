## 1. Brand Identity: WL-STUDIO

### 1.1 Core Definition
*   **Brand Name:** WL-STUDIO
*   **Tagline:** Direct Booking, Zero Commission
*   **Tone of Voice:**
    *   **Perspective:** "Kami" (We/Us) - Collective agency representing a professional team of design & engineering experts.
    *   **Style:** Minimalist, premium, high-confidence, engineering-first, results-driven.
*   **Target Audience:** Villa owners, resort managers, boutique hotel operators, and hospitality brands looking to bypass high OTA (Online Travel Agency) fees.

### 1.2 Core Value Proposition
WL-STUDIO builds high-converting, premium digital portals for hospitality. We combine bespoke visual storytelling (cinematic aesthetics) with robust engineering (PageSpeed 95+, direct booking systems, zero commission transactions) so property owners can own their audience and scale revenue.

## 2. Website Architecture & Specs (Single Page Portal)

### 2.1 Navigation (Navbar)
*   **Logo:** WL-STUDIO (Clean Mohave/Sans typography).
*   **Links:** 
    *   `#home` (Hero)
    *   `#about` (Our Philosophy)
    *   `#services` (Capabilities)
    *   `#work` (Case Studies / Showcases)
    *   `#contact` (Consultation / Direct Line)
*   **Action Button:** "Start Project" (Link to contact).

### 2.2 Section 1: Hero (`#home`)
*   **Component Type:** Glassmorphism Trust Hero.
*   **Visual Assets:** High-resolution dark cinematic background of luxury villas (masked gradient).
*   **Copywriting Layout:**
    *   **Badge:** `Zero Commission` | `Direct Booking Specialist`
    *   **Primary Headline:** 
        ```
        Direct Booking.
        Zero Commission.
        ```
    *   **Subheadline:** 
        "Kami membangun website premium untuk villa & resort yang mengubah pengunjung menjadi pemesanan langsung. Amankan margin Anda, hilangkan komisi OTA."
    *   **Primary CTA:** "Lihat Hasil Karya" (Scroll to `#work`)
    *   **Secondary CTA:** "Watch Showreel" (Play button interaction)
*   **Social Proof Integration:** 
    *   Marquee slider of premium clients / showcase names (Floating Paradise, Karimunjawa, Utamaro, etc.).
    *   Trust metrics card:
        *   `12+` Projects Delivered
        *   `95+` Core Web Vitals (PageSpeed)
        *   `15-25%` OTA Commission Saved

### 2.3 Section 2: About / Philosophy (`#about`)
*   **Objective:** Shift perspective from individual developer to agency capabilities.
*   **Copywriting Angles:**
    *   *"Mengapa Website Penting?"* -> Properti mewah butuh portal digital yang sepadan dengan impresi fisiknya.
    *   *"Mengapa WL-STUDIO?"* -> Kami mengintegrasikan estetika kelas atas dengan fungsionalitas transaksi langsung yang mulus.

### 2.4 Section 3: Services / Capabilities (`#services`)
We structure services into three key pillars:
1.  **Bespoke Design & Storytelling** (Desain visual custom premium, responsif, & interaktif).
2.  **Booking Engine Integration** (Sistem pemesanan langsung tanpa perantara/komisi).
3.  **Search & Speed Optimization** (Optimasi kecepatan tingkat tinggi & SEO untuk jangkauan global).

### 2.5 Section 4: Showcase / Portfolio (`#work`)
*   **Layout:** 3D interactive gallery & grid showcase.
*   **Key Items:** Focus on luxury villa and hospitality projects:
    *   Floating Paradise Villa & Resort
    *   The Secret Karimunjawa
    *   Dancing Mountain Villa
    *   Villa Utamaro
    *   La Beauté Luxury Spa

### 2.6 Section 5: Lead Generation (`#contact`)
*   **Form fields optimized for villa owners:**
    *   Nama Pemilik / Pengelola
    *   Nama Properti (Villa / Resort / Hotel)
    *   Jumlah Unit / Kamar
    *   Metode Booking Saat Ini (Airbnb, Booking.com, WhatsApp, etc.)
    *   Pesan / Kebutuhan khusus
